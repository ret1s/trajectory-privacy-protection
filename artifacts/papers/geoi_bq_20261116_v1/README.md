# GeoI-BQ English paper — author-review package

- [Main manuscript](manuscript.pdf): 17 pages, including references.
- [Supplementary material](supplement.pdf): 14 pages, including detailed proofs, full CIs, selected attackers and extended scenario results.
- [Flat editable LaTeX package](latex_sources.zip): both documents and all required source figures/inputs.
- [Build receipt](build.json), [structural/evidence checks](validation.json), and [visual QA receipt](visual_qa.json).

The paper is separate from the Vietnamese thesis. Its five main sections cover motivation, recent related work, the GeoI-BQ specification and analysis, controlled experiments, and conclusions. Authorship is anonymous for review at the user's request.

The article preserves GeoI-BQ v1, the six-arm paper-adaptation comparison, all 120 retained TEST draws, negative controls and explicit limitations. No defense or attacker was rerun to write the paper. Full-reference access and the stronger public-base control remain central qualifications of the application/utility claim.

The source ZIP was extracted and compiled independently: both PDFs had identical extracted text to their repository builds. All 31 final pages were rendered with Poppler and visually inspected. Mathematical certificates, source pins and evidence binding were checked separately; visual QA is not a mathematical or deployment certificate.

See [paper/submission_notes.md](../../../paper/submission_notes.md) for the provisional JISA direction and author-dependent submission items. Nothing has been submitted to a journal.

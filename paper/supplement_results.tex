\subsection{Tasks and aggregation}
The principal comparison retains the same six implementations, map, catalogue,
service wrapper and time windows. The source has 48 disjoint route families:
20 TRAIN, 8 SELECTION and 20 TEST. Two native trips per family and three TEST
draws yield 120 retained TEST trip-draws per arm. TRAIN and SELECTION have one
retained draw per trip. Bootstrap units are 20 families for the primary tables.
Repeated points and draws do not enlarge that number of independent groups.
The supplementary pair tasks use 10 two-family blocks for S4/S8 and 20
families for S5/S6. Percentile intervals use 10,000 grouped bootstrap replicates.
They condition on the inspected cohort and fitted bank.

S1 observes one service packet; S2 uses the 540--600 s window and targets the
mean of the last four truth coordinates; S3 observes the complete wire and
targets all 31 ticks; S9/S10 target first/last GPS with public boundaries.
S2 is a coordinate proxy, not observed semantic home/work. S4 uses balanced
same-family versus alternative-family association. Physical person/vehicle
labels are unavailable. S5 predicts one of two next edges before/around a
fork; Raw is at chance in this retained cadence. S6 predicts one of two
future destinations in before-fork, turn-window and post-turn phases.
S7 is conditional payload noninterference under a common wrapper. S8 is a
synthetic aligned-partner diagnostic, not collected simultaneous co-travel.

Hit100 and MAE are computed from Euclidean projected error. Within a defined
task, lower Hit and larger MAE favor privacy, without proving resistance to
all estimators. Binary accuracy, Brier score and log loss describe different
properties; a low-accuracy but badly calibrated attacker is not a probability
certificate. Recall uses the same full-catalogue reference/filter/order per
purpose; empty references are N/A. The evaluation returns a full sorted list
to users and uses top five only for scoring.

All additional fake packets remain in their natural windows and costs.
Attackers see public coordinates, order and timing, without Z, true-member
labels or evaluator bookkeeping. Loss-specific SELECTION can choose different
estimators for Hit and MAE. S8 V2 deliberately enlarges the bank after
inspection of V1, so its result is a retained diagnostic, not independent
confirmatory evidence.

\subsection{Original metrics: what can and cannot be transferred}
\input{table_native}
The singleton travel-cost distortion uses eight TRAIN-selected destinations
and their empirical prior, directed road costs, and the travel-cost expression
of TransProtect's Equation 13. It is a local proxy of that contract, not a
reproduction of its original cities, learned predictor or attack.
Q-only GeoI-BQ has no unique replacement coordinate; a best-Q selected using
secret GPS would fabricate a favorable singleton output.

EIE has an expected point-error form, but the paper's VehiTrack observer
is not reproduced by the current bank. We therefore label our observed point
error MAE rather than reporting it as original-paper EIE.
For real-member ASR, the source-calibrated real-member posterior is absent
for the real-plus-dummy adaptations; Q-only GeoI-BQ has no real member by design.
For DER, the original semantic effectiveness/Sim rule and AMap predictor
are unavailable. Source-compatible path counts require full arrival/direction
similarity definitions not recovered in this adaptation. All remain N/A,
with unavailable inputs distinguished from an incompatible output contract.
This is a partial source-metric comparison; it does not claim superiority
on every original metric.

The catalogue has 418 static POIs, with about 91\% acquired in the first
GeoI-BQ packet and 99.49\% in the final session cache. Complete reference
access is already present in the operational prototype factory.
An equally informed offline client could answer all defined static requests
exactly, without establishing zero setup or refresh cost. No measured seventh
arm or operational information-isolation result is invented here.

All 120 protected ledgers permit 11 reads and spend 13--21 units, below the
23-unit cap. This trial therefore tests neither utility after cap exhaustion
nor arbitrary long trips. Correlated sessions need additional composition;
public start/stop is a conditioning assumption. New cities, larger/changing
catalogues, stronger attacker families and a clear proxy--provider information
boundary remain important independent evaluations.
